Refresh Your eBay Store Daily — Automatically

Relist Agent automatically relists your oldest fixed-price listings every day,
helping keep your store fresh and active in eBay's search results. You decide how many listings to relist each day—whether that's 1, 10, 100, or more.
Relist Agent handles the process automatically.

Simple Example

If your store has 600 listings and you relist 10 items per day,
you'll have approximately 60 days of continuous listing rotation. As you add more
inventory, the rotation period grows.

Why Use Relist Agent?

Many eBay sellers believe that maintaining fresh inventory can improve listing exposure and attract
more buyer attention. Instead of manually ending and relisting items every day, Relist Agent automates
the process so you can focus on sourcing, shipping, and growing your business.

Important Cross-Listing Notice

Relist Agent is not designed to work alongside most cross-listing tools.

When an item is relisted, eBay generates a new Item ID and listing URL. Because of this,
cross-listing software that relies on the original Item ID or listing link may lose synchronization
with the newly relisted item.

SOLUTION FOR CROSS-LISTERS:
Relist Agent includes an Exclude feature that lets you select specific items to skip from relisting.
If you use cross-listing software, you can exclude those items from Relist Agent's rotation. This way,
items managed by your cross-lister stay untouched, while your exclusive eBay inventory rotates automatically.
This hybrid approach lets you use both tools together safely.

The Trashed Panda has not tested or researched compatibility with any cross-listing software.
We make no claims regarding compatibility or incompatibility with any specific cross-listing platform.
It is the buyer's responsibility to determine whether Relist Agent will work with their existing
workflow and software tools.

If you actively use cross-listing software, you can test the Exclude feature first—add just a few
items to the exclusion list to verify the workflow works for you before relisting your entire inventory.
