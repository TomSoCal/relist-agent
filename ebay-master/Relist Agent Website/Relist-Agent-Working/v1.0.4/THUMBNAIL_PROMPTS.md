## Image Specifications
- **Dimensions:** 1200x630 pixels (standard social media)
- **Format:** PNG with transparent background (for flexibility)
- **Style:** Professional, modern, minimalist
- **Font:** Bold, sans-serif (Roboto, Open Sans, or similar)
- **Color Palette:** 
  - Primary: Blue (#3498db)
  - Secondary: Green (#27ae60) or Gold (#FFD700)
  - Accent: Orange (#FF8C00)
  - Neutral: White and Dark Gray (#2c3e50)
- **Content:** Clear, benefit-focused, minimal text
- **Target:** eBay sellers, marketplace automation audience

## Usage Notes
- Use consistent branding across all thumbnails
- Include "Relist Agent" logo/text on each image
- Make text large and readable at small sizes (social media)
- Avoid overly complex designs - simple wins in ads
- Test each thumbnail at actual social media sizes
- Include product badge/icon (eBay logo) where appropriate
